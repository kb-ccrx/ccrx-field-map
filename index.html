CCRX Field Map

Reads your Clinics live from Airtable. It needs your own Airtable API key to do that, stored only in this browser, never sent anywhere but Airtable.

  1. Go to airtable.com/create/tokens (on your phone or computer).
  2. Create a token named "Field Map", scope data.records:read, access to base CCRX only.
  3. Copy the token (starts with pat...) and paste it below.

Using this map

Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app:

  1. iPhone: tap the Share icon in Safari, then "Add to Home Screen."
  2. Android: tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome, then "Add to Home screen."

Pins are colored by Stage. Tap a chip to hide/show that stage. Tap a pin for details, directions, and a call button. The refresh icon pulls the latest from Airtable; it also auto-refreshes every 5 minutes while open.

Your location dot only appears after you tap the circle button and allow location access; it is never sent anywhere, it's drawn from your phone's own GPS.
